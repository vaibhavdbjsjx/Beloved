# Competitive landscape (category research)

Research date: 2026-10-01. Sources are public listings and third-party review/marketing
articles — several written by competing apps, so treat claims (ratings, pricing, free tiers)
as indicative, not verified.

## The category

"Pray before you scroll" apps use Apple's Screen Time APIs to place a gate in front of
distracting apps; the gate is unlocked by a spiritual action.

| App | Unlock mechanic | Faith scope | Notes (as reported) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **prayer lock: christian focus** (Covenant Studios) — listing no longer resolves (iTunes API, 2026-10-01) | mood-matched short prayer; reportedly unlocks for the rest of the day, resets at midnight | Christian | streaks, prayer analytics, subscription; reviewers disagree on whether a meaningful free tier exists; no schedules/widgets reported |
| Bible Mode | scan a physical Bible or read a verse | Christian | free + IAP |
| FaithLock | Bible verse + quiz | Christian | BSB verses |
| Holy Focus | prayer + verse display | Christian | passive; easy to skim |
| Sanctum | "Sacred Pause", mood-based verse, journaling, AI chat | Christian | blocking secondary |
| Pray Screen Time / Pray Focus / Bible Pause / GodTime (Android) | prayer or reflection prompt | Christian | varying polish |

Sources: [getfaithlock.com comparison](https://www.getfaithlock.com/resources/best-christian-app-blocker), [psalmo.app review](https://psalmo.app/blog/prayer-lock-app-review), [psalmo.app guide](https://psalmo.app/blog/block-apps-until-you-pray), [Google Play listings](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.maubaron.prayerlock).

**Update 2026-10-01 (iTunes Search API):** the "prayer lock: christian focus" listing no longer
resolves in US/IN/GB storefronts. Faith-specific blockers exist beyond Christianity:
Islam — DeenLock: Prayer Focus, Salat Guard, prayer lock islam, prayer lock: muslim focus,
Prayer Lock: Noor; Hinduism — Dharma Lock: Hindu Prayer App. "Multi-faith" alone is therefore
not a moat: the differentiation must come from quality, trust (provenance), privacy, and
honest, reliable blocking.

Shared mechanics: app selection, prayer interruption, mood selection, personalised prayers,
streaks, analytics, subscriptions. General screen-time blockers (one sec, Opal, Jomo…) offer
stronger blocking UX but no spiritual layer.

## Gaps we can own (without cloning anyone)

1. **Multi-faith by architecture** — Islam and Hinduism with their own structures (dua/dhikr,
   mantra/deity focus), calendars, and sensitivities; faith-neutral path for seekers.
2. **Trustworthy content** — visible provenance on every line; reviewed and licensed sources;
   AI never passes as scripture.
3. **Privacy as a feature** — no account, on-device history, minimal data.
4. **Context awareness** — religious calendar (Lent, Ramadan, festivals), time of day, mood,
   opt-in customs — explained, not imposed.
5. **Honest, flexible unlocks** — chosen durations (incl. short "minutes of use"), not
   all-day-only; clear about iOS limits.
6. **Healthy habit design** — consistency over streak pressure; no manipulative mechanics.
7. **Free core** — the pause itself should work without paying; premium adds depth.
8. **Later**: assistant grounded in sources, journal, multiple languages/regions, schedules,
   widgets, Focus filter integration.

## Things we must not do

Copy names, icons (orange lock), copy text, prayer texts, or UI of existing apps; use
"Prayer Lock" as the public App Store name.
