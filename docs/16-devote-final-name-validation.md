## 2. Market conflict research — summary

**Finding: Devote has serious, active conflicts in the same product category.** At least five
distinct religious/devotional products already use "Devote" as their brand. The strongest is an
established, monetised Christian Bible app with an AI question-answering feature. That
overlaps directly with our planned Bible content, AI spiritual assistant, and devotional
moments.

**What the search did *not* find:** a live US trademark registration for DEVOTE in the software
(9/42), education/religious (41), or personal/spiritual-services (45) classes, and no
registration by the main app competitor.

So the name is not blocked by any registered US mark found here. Whether prior unregistered
use by these products creates rights against us, and how much confusion risk is acceptable,
is a legal and commercial judgement **requiring professional clearance before Apple
identifiers are created.**

## 7. Multi-faith brand fit (naming-language analysis; not user research; no believer represented)

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Communicates devotion? | Yes — directly. It also carries a second meaning that fits the product: *devote your attention* (intentional use of time and screens) |
| Christian users | Natural. But "devotional" is an established **Christian app genre**, and several Christian apps are already named "Devote" — likely to be read as a Christian Bible/devotional app unless the descriptor says otherwise |
| Hindu users | Very natural — devotion = *bhakti*; Indian English uses "devotional" and "devotee" widely for Hindu practice; "Dev" (deity) at the start of the word may feel familiar to Hindi speakers |
| Muslim users | Acceptable but less native. Everyday Muslim English favours "worship", *ibadah*, *dhikr*, *dua*; "devotion" appears in translations and in existing apps ("Devoted - Shia Prayer App", "Shia Devotions"). No disrespectful reading found |
| Restricted to prayer? | No — devotion spans prayer, mantra, dua, scripture, meditation, reflection, habits |
| Expansion | Supports meditation, scripture study, reflection, journals, education, family; extends to Judaism, Sikhism, Buddhism without sounding awkward |
| Premium | Short (6 letters), single verb — the convention of premium faith apps (Abide, Hallow, Glorify). That convention is also why the word is crowded |
| Pronunciation / spelling | Easy: *dih-VOHT*; one obvious spelling |

## 9. App Store positioning (proposals only — no metadata changed)

Apple's limits (App Store Connect Help, checked 2026-10-01): **App name 2–30 characters;
subtitle up to 30 characters.** Name uniqueness can only be confirmed inside App Store Connect.

Because three apps already use `Devote: …` with Bible/devotional descriptors, the descriptor
should **separate** this product from them. Name it by faiths or by function, never by "Bible"
or "Devotional(s)":

| Structure | Name (length) | Subtitle (length) |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-faith descriptor | `Devote: Prayer, Mantra & Dua` (28) | `A prayerful pause before apps` (29) |
| Function descriptor | `Devote: Prayerful Screen Time` (29) | `Prayer, mantra & dua moments` (28) |
| Category descriptor | `Devote: Spiritual Wellbeing` (27) | `Devote your attention first` (27) |
| Bare brand | `Devote` (6) | — most likely to be confused with the existing Devote apps; availability unknown |

Avoid next to "Devote": "Bible", "Bible Study", "Devotional(s)", "Devotions", "Daily", "Chat",
"Companion" (all in existing Devote titles), "Prayer & Focus" (25 existing listings), "Prayer
Lock", "Christian Focus".

## 10. Technical identity proposal (NOT applied)

```
com.<owner-supplied-reverse-dns>.devote                         app
com.<owner-supplied-reverse-dns>.devote.ShieldConfiguration     extension
com.<owner-supplied-reverse-dns>.devote.ShieldAction            extension
com.<owner-supplied-reverse-dns>.devote.ActivityMonitor         extension
com.<owner-supplied-reverse-dns>.devote.UITests                 UI tests
group.com.<owner-supplied-reverse-dns>.devote                   App Group
```
- Applying it later means changing **one line** — `PL_BUNDLE_ID_PREFIX` in
  `Config/Identity.xcconfig`; the extension IDs and App Group derive from it.
- Do **not** use a prefix that implies another party's namespace (e.g. `com.devote`,
  `com.joindevote`, `com.joinrisen`).
- Bundle IDs are **permanent** after the first App Store release; app names can change. Bundle
  IDs are not shown to users, but if legal clearance later forces a brand change, a
  `…devote` ID would stay forever. To proceed before clearance, the owner can choose a neutral
  technical slug instead (a technical choice, not a brand name).
- Still required from the owner: (1) reverse-DNS prefix they control, (2) Team ID, (3) paid
  Apple Developer Program account (individual or organization — the seller name is shown on the
  App Store), (4) go/no-go after clearance.

## 11. Risks (honest)

1. **Same-category confusion and possible legal claims.** Multiple active religious apps named
   "Devote" (US, UK, **India**), including Risen Ministries' monetised app with AI Q&A and
   overlapping features. Possible outcomes include user confusion, review friction under App
   Store Guidelines 4.1 (copycats) / 2.3.7 (names), and an IP complaint from a prior user. None
   of these was tested here.
2. **India class 9 "Devot" registration** could affect an Indian software filing.
3. **Searchability is weak.** A common English verb, a store flooded with "devotional" apps,
   and several existing "Devote" apps; owning search results for "Devote" is unlikely.
4. **Christian-coded perception.** "Devotional" reads as a Christian genre; existing Devote apps
   are all Christian. Counter it with the multi-faith descriptor and screenshots.
5. **Domain/social ecosystem.** The exact `.com`/`.app`/`.in` and `@devote` handles aren't
   available as-is (`.app` and `.in` are for sale). Composite domains such as `devotehq.com`,
   `getdevote.app` look available today.
6. **Unsearched registers.** EU, UK, and WIPO weren't searched; US/India data came from
   third-party mirrors that can lag the official registers.
