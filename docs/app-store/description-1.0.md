## Description (max 4000)

Beloved is a daily companion for Christians, Muslims and Hindus. Prayers, mantras and duas come word for word from public-domain and openly licensed sources, shown with their source. Nothing sacred is written by AI.

HINDU
• Choose who you pray to today — Ganesha, Shiva, Vishnu, Balaji, Krishna, Rama, Hanuman, Devi, Durga, Lakshmi, Saraswati, Murugan, Surya or the Navagraha.
• Mantras and stotras for every purpose — study, prosperity, health, protection, new beginnings — in Sanskrit with transliteration and meaning.
• A 108-bead japa mala with soft haptics.
• Festival dates from the Government of India calendar, with a greeting on the day and step-by-step guides for pujas, vrats and aarti.
• The Bhagavad Gita in Sanskrit, transliteration and English, with an 18-day reading plan.

MUSLIM
• Accurate prayer times calculated on your iPhone, with a countdown, reminders and seven calculation methods.
• Qibla compass, tasbih counter, Hijri date, a Ramadan suhoor and iftar countdown and a zakat calculator.
• Duas from the Qur'an with Arabic and meaning, and the Qur'an in Arabic and English with a 30-day khatm plan.
• Guides for wudu, salah, Jumu'ah and the blessed days of the year.

CHRISTIAN
• The prayers every Christian knows, and a guided Rosary with the day's mysteries and their Gospel passages.
• The Bible (World English Bible) with plans for the Gospels, Psalms and the New Testament.
• Feasts and seasons — Advent, Lent, Easter — with guides to keep them.
• A prayer journal for requests and answered prayers.

A COMPANION WHO KNOWS YOUR TRADITION
Ask about stories, history, festivals or how to do a puja, prayer or fast, and get a clear, careful answer in your own tradition. Replies are written by AI and labelled as such. Free: 10 questions a day. Premium: 100.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. Your prayers, journal and location stay on your iPhone, and sync only to your own iCloud so they come back on a new phone. Sign in with Apple is optional.

BELOVED PREMIUM
Free: scripture, prayer times, Qibla, your beads, short mantras and core prayers, festival greetings. Premium ($5.99 a month or $34.99 a year, 7 days free): 100 companion questions a day, every long stotra, the guided Rosary, all reading plans and an unlimited journal. Payment is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
